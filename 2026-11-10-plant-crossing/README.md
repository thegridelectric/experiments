# plant-crossing, 2026-11-10

> What this is: the first crossing of the two sim plant words over the
> dev broker (OPS-40). A plant in `gridworks-terminalasset` on the AMQP
> face, the scada's plant link on the MQTT face, an independent witness
> beside it. Does `sim.plant.flux` reach the link with every sensed
> channel of the no-sieg House0 fixture and none extra, and does a
> `sim.plant.actuation` make the return trip? Verdict: PASS, on the
> existing wrapped `gw` grammar with no broker topology change.

## Why

A simulated house is to reach the scada over the broker and nowhere else,
speaking channel names and relay node names only. Two things could not be
settled by reading code. Whether a gwbase actor (AMQP) and the scada's
own paho client (MQTT) can exchange these words at all without a new
exchange or a new routing class, and whether the two repos, each reading
the same layout file with its own code, agree on which channels the
plant feeds. The plant link's unit tests share one process and one
codec; they cannot see either.

## Setup

Laptop, `d1` universe, `gw-dev-rabbit` (AMQP 5672, MQTT 1885, vhost
`d1__1`), up four days. Three parties, all in `run.sh`:

- **The plant**: `ta-plant` from `gridworks-terminalasset` (`70bb07f`),
  `--flux-seconds 2 --seed 20261110`, on the fixture layout
  `tests/config/gw.house0.no.sieg.hazel.layout.json`, identity
  `g.node.gt.json` in this folder (alias
  `d1.isone.ct.newhaven.hazel1.ta`, written by the plant from the
  layout). A drifting plant: each channel on a bounded random walk; it
  logs the actuations it hears and does not act on them.
- **The scada's end**: `crossing.py`, run in `gridworks-scada`'s venv
  (`3426897b`). Not a scada: a `BrokerPlantLink` alone, built from the
  same fixture's aliases, with one sensor claiming every channel the
  scada's layout loader says a simulated device captures. It holds
  `store-pump-relay` at Energize before it has a connection, waits for
  four fluxes, sets DeEnergize, waits two seconds.
- **The witness**: a paho client of its own on `gw/#`, decoding every
  payload on a `sim-plant` topic through the scada's vendored sema
  snapshot codec.

Both snapshots were vendored with staging allowed from sema `862b7c1`;
neither word is published. Nothing deployed was touched.

## Found

**PASS**, all six checks (`logs/crossing.json`).

- **The words cross both ways with no topology change.** Flux on
  `gw/d1-isone-ct-newhaven-hazel1-ta/to/d1-isone-ct-newhaven-hazel1-scada/sim-plant-flux`,
  actuation on the reverse pair ending `sim-plant-actuation`: the
  wrapped `gw` grammar on `amq.topic`, MQTT slashes for AMQP dots.
- **The two repos agree on the channel set.** 35 channels expected by
  the scada's layout loader, 35 in every flux at the link and at the
  witness, none extra, none unsensed.
- **Every payload decodes.** Five fluxes at the link, six at the
  witness, nothing undecodable through the snapshot codec.
- **Values drift.** All 35 channels changed value across the run.
- **A position held before the connection reaches the plant.** The
  Energize set before the link connected was never published (paho
  drops a publish with no connection). The plant heard it twice all the
  same, restated once on connect and once on the first flux, then the
  DeEnergize 6 s later.

## Analysis notes

- A first pass at 11:16 UTC, driven by hand against the plant link
  before it was committed, gave the same verdict and the same three
  actuations. Its logs were replaced by this scripted run on committed
  code; the committed link differs from that tree by nothing.
- The double Energize is the link's two healing paths firing together
  at start, not a duplicate send of one command.
- This is the link alone, not a scada. No actor consumed a value and no
  relay actor sent the actuation.
- The plant here sends every sensed channel every period. The June
  in-process run (`../2026-06-12-sim-plant-flux/`) gated flux on async
  change; that gate is not in this plant.
- The plant stamps the flux read time from the wall clock.
- A plant restart was not tried. A plant silent for less than the
  link's 15 s threshold is not restated to by the flux path.
- A `capture_broker.py` belonging to another experiment
  (`../2026-11-05-flo-dev-harness/captures`) was running on the same
  broker; this run's traffic is in that capture.

## Folder contents & experimental method

All data here was GENERATED by this run on the laptop's dev broker; none
of it is in the journal DB or the eventstore, and a re-run produces a
new dataset. The run started and stopped one laptop process (the plant)
and touched nothing deployed.

- `README.md`.
- `run.sh`: the whole run, wall clock per step. Needs `gw-dev-rabbit`
  up and the sibling `gridworks-scada` and `gridworks-terminalasset`
  checkouts on the commits under test.
- `crossing.py`: the scada's end and the witness; prints the verdict.
- `emit_instances.py`: the run record from the logs.
- `g.node.gt.json`: the plant's dev identity, as the plant wrote it.
- `logs/clock.txt`, `logs/code-under-test.txt` (each repo's HEAD and
  dirty-file count at the start), `logs/plant.log`,
  `logs/crossing.json` (the verdict), `logs/crossing.stderr` (empty).
- `instances/plant.crossing-gw.experiment.run-001.json`.

Regenerate everything from scratch:

    ./2026-11-10-plant-crossing/run.sh
    uv run python 2026-11-10-plant-crossing/emit_instances.py

No readings instance is kept, so there is no display CSV.
