# spruce-plant-seam-roll, 2026-11-18

> What this is: spruce rolled from scada main (7e8e52f4) to `jm/plant-seam`
> (72c00fa3, PR 630) for a bounded window, watched for the device-path
> glitches the plant seam could introduce; verdict PASS, PR 630 merged and
> the fleet rolled to the merged main the same evening.

## Why

PR 630 reshapes the path from device actors to their hardware. The scada
protocol's sculpt rule asks for a run on a real box before merge, and
spruce has the fleet's one GPIO relay (OPS-692).

## Setup

Spruce on scada main 7e8e52f4 with its LTN on the same SHA, service
`gwspaceheat` up 7 days, log clean. `scripts/roll.sh spruce to jm/plant-seam`
run from the laptop at 19:14 ET took both boxes to 72c00fa3 on the tlayouts
main pair. Two read-only listeners ran from the laptop for the window: the
pi's service journal over ssh and the tap broker bound to spruce's scada
and LTN aliases.

## Found

- **Boot clean on the branch.** No traceback. LocalControl, hp-boss, five
  GPIO sensors, the gw108 thermistor reader, the relays' boot-adopt and the
  power meter all started; the one GPIO relay (`vdc-relay`, GPIO 23)
  energized and de-energized under the pico cycler exactly as in the
  2026-10-22 boot on the previous line.
- **Every relay and power channel reports** in the post-roll snapshot with
  the same values as before the roll.
- **Four `unserved-post` warnings** at 19:15:21 to 19:15:23 are pico
  `code-update` posts after their cycler reboot; the 2026-10-22 boot log has
  the same posts. Not a device-path fault.
- **PASS.** 45 minutes on the branch, then the merged main: no traceback,
  no device-path glitch, the GPIO relay actuated twice under the cycler
  with the picos rebooting each time, every channel reporting.

## Analysis notes

Two snapshot differences after the roll are restart artifacts and the
same on main, whose `scada.py` differs from the branch only in the
plant-link start and stop:

- `zone2-living-rm-set` and `zone4-garage-set` are derived setpoints the
  derived-generator publishes only once it has seen a heat call end
  (`phase=lastheatcallendtemp`); after a restart it sits in
  `Unknown`/`Suspect...` until then.
- The `auto` machine is absent from `LatestStateList` because the scada
  seeds only its own TopState at boot and the auto machine's state is
  recorded on a transition (`scada.py` `initialize_hierarchical_state_data`);
  the pre-roll entry had a 19:09:58 transition behind it.
- The GPIO `RuntimeWarning: This channel is already in use` on the relay
  pin setup also appears in the 2026-10-22 boot log on the previous line.

## Folder contents & experimental method

All data GENERATED by this experiment's own listeners; nothing pulled from
the journal DB. The fleet traffic the tap heard is also in the journal.

- `runbook.sh`: the commands, in order.
- `evidence/pre-roll-tail.log`: the service journal before the roll.
- `evidence/spruce-journal.log`, `evidence/beech-journal.log`: ssh
  `journalctl` follows of the box services through the window.
- `evidence/tap-stdout.log`, `evidence/tap-fleet-stdout.log`: `tap_listen.py`
  stdout (read-only tap broker), spruce only and then the fleet roll.
- `evidence/tap-capture-20261118-191232.jsonl` (3.4 MB, spruce window) and
  `evidence/tap-capture-20261118-194918.jsonl` (19.6 MB, fleet roll): the
  full captures, untouched. Over the 2 MB cap, so not committed: they go
  to the immutable store (key to be named here) and the folder commits
  their reduction, `evidence/tap-capture-*-rows.jsonl`, one row per
  message (receive time, routing key, TypeName, Version, bytes):

      uv run python 2026-11-18-spruce-plant-seam-roll/reduce_capture.py

- `emit_instances.py`: writes
  `instances/hw1.isone.me.versant.keene.spruce.scada-gw.experiment.run-001.json`,
  the verdict, through the gwexp snapshot and reads it back:

      uv run python 2026-11-18-spruce-plant-seam-roll/emit_instances.py

No `gw.readings` instance: the readings of the window are in the journal.

## The fleet roll to the merged main

PR 630 merged to main as 2b2028f0 at about 19:55 ET (after 4b65110d, which
quiets the `code-update` posts, and 1c8f686c, which drops the relay pin's
in-use warning). `scripts/roll.sh <house> to main` then took every house
and its LTN to 2b2028f0, in the order beech, oak, elm, spruce, fir, maple,
between 19:58 and 20:02 ET. Every pi booted with no traceback. Maple, elm
and fir took the regenerated operational params at the roll: their boxes
had `flo.tuning.bruce` 001 and tlayouts main has 002 with the unmet-load
penalty on, which main refuses to boot without.

Two patterns in the event stream that are not faults:

- A house with a secondary box (beech, elm, fir, maple) shows two
  startup/shutdown pairs a few seconds apart: the primary pi and the
  secondary each restart once.
- Spruce's 20:00:00 shutdown event arrived twice, live at 20:00:00 and
  again at 20:00:08 after the restart, with the same `TimeCreatedMs`: the
  stop came before the ack and the persisted copy was re-uploaded.

The 21:00 FLO cycle on the new code: every LTN decoded the FLO's
`flo.recommendation` 002 with no error (the pre-roll LTNs had refused it,
expecting 001); beech took contract 5bc and oak contract 67d, so oak
returned to its LTN after the 20:00 hour it spent in LocalControl because
its LTN restarted 45 s before that hour's slot and its FLO request was
lost with the stopped process. Elm, fir, maple and spruce are monitor-only
LTNs and log the recommendation as dropped with no request held, as they
did before the roll. Lesson for the roll how-to: do not roll an LTN house
in the last four minutes before the hour; the FLO ask goes out at three.
