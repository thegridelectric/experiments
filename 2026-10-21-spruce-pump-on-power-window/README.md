# spruce-pump-on-power-window, 2026-10-21

> What this is: an 11 minute spruce window of `jm/spruce-unlimbo` at
> `a9acd401` plus the pump-rule fix, to read the secondary pump follow the
> heat pump's measured power under admin. Verdict in Found; the logbook
> entry is the index record.

## Why

The secondary pump moved to hp-boss after the 2026-10-18 window (the
pump floated with the tree and froze under admin). The first cut of
hp-boss's rule ORed the call in: pump on while called. That is not the
Nolan rule. We do not trust the primary pump in either direction, so the
secondary pump runs on the Samsung's measured power alone, through
hp-watch: on above the on line, off below the off line, on while
unknown. The suite pins it
(`tests/actors/test_hp_watch_live.py`
`test_under_admin_the_pump_follows_the_heat_pumps_power_not_the_call`);
this window reads it on the real relay and the real unit.

## Setup

Spruce, window scada from `~/gridworks-scada-unlimbo` at `a9acd401`
(the pump-rule fix is in the working tree of the laptop checkout and
committed in that SHA), window pair placed by `put_layout.sh spruce
hp-boss-pump` (layout put, ops identical). Experiment window: the
winter hack and gwspaceheat stopped for the bound and restored by the
script at exit. The operator takes admin from `gwa watch spruce` and
turns the heat pump on through hp-boss.

Pre-flight: the run proves nothing if the compressor does not cross the
on line inside the bound, so the heat pump is turned on early; the
house is at 71.5 F, so an 11 minute displacement of the hack is safe.

## Found

**PASS.** Six pump reads in the boot log, every one on the watch and
none on the call:

| ET | event | pump command |
|---|---|---|
| 16:24:17.304 | boot, watch Unknown | CloseRelay |
| 16:24:17.889 | watch Unknown -> HpDetectedOff | OpenRelay |
| 16:27:08.808 | admin TurnOn, call relay closes | none |
| 16:27:39.918 | watch HpDetectedOff -> HpDetectedOn | CloseRelay |
| 16:28:05.153 | admin TurnOff, call relay opens | none |
| 16:32:33.004 | watch HpDetectedOn -> HpDetectedOff | OpenRelay |

Each pump command followed its watch transition by 4 to 5 ms. The
Samsung took 31 s from the call closing to cross the on line and 4 min
28 s from the call opening to drop below the off line; the pump ran
through the whole wind-down. The admin timeout at 16:33:05 handed the
tree back to local control with the call and the pump already open, so
the handover moved no relay.

Also seen, none of it the claim under test: a Critical
`i2c-relay-actuation-failed` on the pump relay at boot (closed 40 s
while told open, 75 W on the pump channel), eight `i2c-relay-drift`
Warnings at 16:24:57, a Critical `i2c-expander-reset` on 0x21 at
16:25:17 (the OPS-452 signature), the four zone setpoint channels never
valued, the iso valve closed under admin while the heat pump ran,
`secondary-pump-ct` flat at 168, and a 4 min 28 s run past the call
opening. The spoke's field section has the detail.

## Analysis notes

The boot log prints watch transitions and hp-boss commands but no
hp-odu power values; the power series is in the broker capture
(`hp-odu-pwr` readings). The 4 min 28 s wind-down is one sample of the
Samsung's stop behaviour on a short run, not a characterisation.

## Folder contents & experimental method

Everything here was GENERATED by this window: the box's boot log and the
laptop's broker capture, copied by `spruce_window.sh off`. The window
stopped the plant services for its bound. Nothing is from the journal
DB.

- `README.md` — this file.
- `runbook.sh` — the commands as run.
- `evidence/spruce-window-boot.log` — the box's `/tmp/spruce-window/boot.log`, verbatim, provenance header on top.
- `evidence/broker-capture-20261021-162405.jsonl` and its `.provenance.txt` — the laptop's dev-broker capture for the window (61 messages), copied from `scratch/`.
- `evidence/events/` — the 11 events persisted on the box during the window (3 `report.event`), pulled by `spruce_window.sh off`.
- `instances/gw.experiment.run-001.json` — the run word, emitted by `emit_instances.py` from the boot log's first and last stamps.

    uv run python 2026-10-21-spruce-pump-on-power-window/emit_instances.py
