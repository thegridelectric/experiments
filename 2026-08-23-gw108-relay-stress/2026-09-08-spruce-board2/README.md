# spruce-board2, 2026-09-08 (gw108-relay-stress run 2)

> What this is: does the 0x21 expander reset on iso-valve energize
> (`../2026-08-23-spruce/`, run B: 35 resets in 100 toggles)
> follow the gw108 board, or the house? Same harness, same runs, a
> replaced board. Verdict: the reset follows the house, not the board:
> B2 17 resets in 100 toggles on the new board (08-23: 35), still none
> with two or more other coils on (F2, A2: 0/60).

## Why

Joe could not reproduce the reset on his bench gw108s, so George
replaced spruce's board today. If the reset was the board's, run B on
the new one should show none; if it is the house side (the iso valve
actuator's inrush on the relay supply, the wiring), run B reproduces at
about the 08-23 rate and the electrical question stays open on the
house, not the board. Runs F and A are the guards: they were clean on
08-23 and must stay clean.

## Setup

Harness: `../relay_stress.py`, run on
spruce from the box's `~/experiments` checkout, exactly as its README
"Folder contents & experimental method" gives the commands, with new
labels. Single writer on 0x21 for the window: `spruce-summer-hack`,
`gwspaceheat` and its restart timer stopped (already stopped for the
day's admin-panel window), the admin-panel window scada stopped for the
run and the panel quit, and restarted afterwards. Charge valve OFF
throughout; the DAC untouched; run B has the secondary pump off for
about 2.5 min.

    cd ~/experiments/2026-08-23-gw108-relay-stress
    CHARGE_POSTURE=0 PUMP_POSTURE=0 HP_POSTURE=0 LOADS=0 TARGET=iso PERIODS=1 TOGGLES=100 MAX_RESETS=1000 python relay_stress.py --run B2 --yes
    CHARGE_POSTURE=0 PUMP_POSTURE=1 HP_POSTURE=1 LOADS=0 TARGET=iso PERIODS=1 TOGGLES=30 MAX_RESETS=1000 python relay_stress.py --run F2 --yes
    CHARGE_POSTURE=0 PUMP_POSTURE=1 HP_POSTURE=1 LOADS=5 TARGET=iso PERIODS=1 TOGGLES=30 MAX_RESETS=1000 python relay_stress.py --run A2 --yes

Bar: B2 resets / 100 against the 08-23 rate of 35 / 100. Zero on the
new board and the reset was the board's; the 08-23 rate and it is the
house's.

## Found

**The new gw108 resets on the same condition. Energizing-or-de-energizing
the iso-valve relay with no other 0x21 coil on reset the chip 17 times in
100 toggles (08-23 old board: 35/100); with the secondary pump and
hp-call on, 0/30; with those plus the five unwired loads, 0/30. The
electrical question is on the house side of the relay: the iso valve
actuator on the relay supply, or that relay position's wiring.**

| run | other coils | toggles | resets | at iso→1 / iso→0 | 08-23 twin |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| B2 | none | 100 | **17** | 3 / 14 | B: 35, 33 / 2 |
| F2 | secondary pump + hp-call | 30 | **0** | — | F: 0 |
| A2 | pump, hp-call, 5 unwired | 30 | **0** | — | A: 0 |

- The rate halved and the edge flipped: the old board tripped almost
  only on energize, this one almost only on de-energize (14 of 17). Same
  mechanism (a transient on the iso relay's switching, survivable only
  with other coils drawing), different board tolerances; a board swap
  does not close it.
- The two-coil guard holds on the new board: the software rule from
  08-23 stands (never switch the iso relay with fewer than two other
  0x21 coils energized).
- No reset in the day's admin-panel window before the run, during which
  the iso relay was switched only with the pump on.
